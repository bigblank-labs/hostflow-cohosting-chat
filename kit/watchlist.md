# Watchlist — Hostflow

> Project: Hostflow digital products (STR cohosting)
> Brand URL: https://hostflow.me
> Created: 2026-09-15 by Snow
> Updated: 2026-09-15

## ICP (the one-line description)

Aspiring or new short-term-rental **co-hosts** — they want to manage other people's
properties (not own). They've either had a bad guest, hit capacity managing their
own listing, or realized the income ceiling for solo hosts. They Google things like
"how to become an Airbnb cohost", "cohost contract template", "Airbnb cohost pricing".

## Top 5 competitors

1. **Airbnb Co-host Network** — built-in marketplace, free to join; takes 10% of
   booking subtotal. Threat: low (it's a directory, not a course).
2. **STR Insights / ScaleYourSTR** — Sam Zellner's podcast + paid mentorship at
   $2k-5k. Threat: high (already owns the audience).
3. **Cohort-based courses** (various) — BnbFormulas, HostLikeaBoss, etc. $300-900.
   Threat: medium (saturated but buyers exist).
4. **Local cohost agencies** — full-service 25-30% of revenue. Threat: low for
   digital products, but their existence validates the demand.
5. **YouTube creators** (free cohosting content) — threat: low for buyers at
   $200 but high for "I'm just researching" searches.

## Price band

- Cohosting Startup Kit: **$200 flat** (PDF lessons bundle) — Josh's directive.
- Mentorship course: **$1,000** (cohort-based) — Josh's directive.
- Future: $150-200 tests if we add small-ticket offers.
- Anchored against STR mentorship market ($300-900 cohort, $2k-5k high-ticket).

## Forbidden claims

- No income guarantees. ("may support", "designed to" only.)
- No "passive income" language. (Cohosting is work.)
- No "Airbnb approved" / "Airbnb certified" (Airbnb does not certify).
- No "100% booked" / "always full" implied outcomes.
- No fake testimonials or "70,000+ users" claims (per earlier feedback).

## Brand voice reminders

- Hostflow live brand: "Co-hosting Done Right." Premium, calm, owner-voice.
- Snow Studio system voice: no exclamation, no emoji, mono labels, hedged.
- For Hostflow digital products: think mentor talking to a serious host. Confident,
  practical, no hype. "I've done this. Here are the documents I wish I had."

## Standing metrics to track
