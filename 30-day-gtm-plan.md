# HOSTFLOW — 30-Day Go-to-Market Plan

**Brief:** HOSTFLOW-002 (STR Launch Line)
**Author:** CMO + Strat
**Date:** 2026-09-17
**Goal:** 100 free PDF downloads + 3 $49 purchases in 14 days, with the funnel validated for scaling by Day 30.

---

## Phase 1: Days 1-7 (Launch + Test)

### Goals
- Get the funnel live and reachable
- Get first 30 leads on the free PDF
- Validate the landing page converts at >40% opt-in rate

### Activities

**Day 1-2: Foundation**
- Stripe Payment Link for $49 STR Starter System (Josh provides account)
- Resend or Klaviyo signup for free PDF email capture
- Update hostflow.me hero: replace "Book a Call" CTA with "Get the free prompt pack →" (still keep "Book a Call" as secondary CTA for service clients)
- Update chat V3 recommendation: surface the FREE 21 Prompts as the warm-up offer, Kit/Mentorship as upsells

**Day 3-4: First distribution push**
- IG post: "21 AI prompts I use to manage my Airbnb" (carousel showing 5 prompts, swipeable)
- Reddit post (r/AirbnbHosts): "I built a free PDF of 21 AI prompts for STR hosts. Sharing it free."
- Twitter/X thread: "21 AI prompts for Airbnb co-hosts. Here's #1: ..." (one prompt per tweet, thread of 21)

**Day 5-7: Iterate**
- Read conversion data
- A/B test the IG carousel hook ("21 prompts" vs "The prompts I use" vs "Steal these prompts")
- If Reddit post is removed, post a value-add comment on similar threads instead
- Send the first email to free PDF downloaders: "Welcome. Here's how to use the prompts + if you want more, here's the $49 system"

### KPIs

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Free PDF downloads (Day 1-7) | 30+ |
| Free → $49 conversion (Day 1-7) | 0 conversions expected; goal is just leads |
| Email open rate | >35% |
| Landing page → free PDF | >40% |

---

## Phase 2: Days 8-14 (Scale what's working)

### Goals
- Reach 100 cumulative free leads
- Get first 1-3 $49 purchases
- Identify the channel with the highest conversion

### Activities

- **Double down** on whichever channel from Phase 1 produced the most qualified leads (likely IG or Reddit)
- **Add paid traffic** if organic is working: $50-100 Meta ad spend testing IG-as-source → landing → free PDF
- **Add a second IG content format:** Reels walking through each prompt in the wild (15-30s Reels, one per day for 14 days)
- **Pitch 1-2 micro-influencers** in the STR / cohosting space (10-50k followers) for free product seeding

### KPIs

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Free PDF cumulative | 100 |
| $49 purchases | 1-3 |
| Cost per free lead | <$1 if running ads |
| $49 conversion rate | 2-5% of free leads |

---

## Phase 3: Days 15-30 (Optimize + cross-sell)

### Goals
- Reach 3+ $49 purchases total
- Begin cross-selling to existing $200 Kit buyers (upsell pitch)
- Set up the email nurture for $49 → $200 Kit

### Activities

- **Nurture email sequence** kicks in: $49 buyers get 4-week email pitch to upgrade to $200 Kit (positioned as "now that you've started, here's how to scale")
- **Re-engage free PDF leads** who haven't purchased: send "you got the prompts, here's the system to actually use them at scale" pitch
- **Add a second lead magnet** to test (optional): "5 listing-photo mistakes that kill your bookings" — different hook, different ICP slice
- **Begin building the $200 Kit upgrade path:** if a $49 buyer hits the email sequence and converts to $200, document the path

### KPIs

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Free PDF cumulative | 250+ |
| $49 purchases cumulative | 5-10 |
| $49 → $200 Kit conversion | 1-2 |
| Refund rate | <5% |
| Email unsubscribe rate | <2% |

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Reddit bans the post | Have 2-3 alt subreddits queued + comment-seeding on similar threads |
| IG shadow-bans the carousel | Use 3 different IG accounts to test; post at different times |
| Stripe takes >24h to set up | Lead with free PDF only for first 48h; $49 button goes live the moment Stripe is ready |
| Email provider not picked yet | Use Resend (free tier, 1-day setup); switch to Klaviyo later if scale demands it |
| Founder bandwidth (Josh is also doing the launch) | Front-load the first 7 days; reduce to maintenance mode by Day 14 |
| YouTube reference can't be researched | Working from public knowledge; can verify manually if needed |

## Decision gates

- **Day 7:** If <20 free leads → channel is wrong, pivot to Reddit + IG comment-seeding
- **Day 14:** If $49 conversion <1% → landing page needs work; pause paid traffic
- **Day 21:** If $49 → $200 Kit cross-sell not working → email sequence needs redesign
- **Day 30:** If <3 $49 sales → re-evaluate the entire funnel (likely pricing or ICP problem)
