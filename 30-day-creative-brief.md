# HOSTFLOW — 30-Day Creative Brief + Content Plan

**Brief:** HOSTFLOW-002 (STR Launch Line)
**Author:** CMO + Market
**Date:** 2026-09-17
**Goal:** Ship the visual + copy assets for all 30 days of the launch line.

---

## Brand voice guardrails (carried from Brand Book v1.0)

- **No exclamation marks.** Anywhere.
- **No emoji.** Including the AI prompts PDF cover, IG captions, email subject lines.
- **Hedged claims only.** "May support", "designed to", "in our experience" — never "guaranteed", "always", "100%".
- **No fake testimonials.** No "70,000+ hosts" invented numbers. No fabricated quotes.
- **Premium, calm, mentor-talking-to-serious-host tone.** Confident without hype.
- **Mono labels for any doses / numbers** (not really applicable here, but kept for system consistency).

## Forbidden claims

- "Guaranteed income", "passive income", "100% booked", "Airbnb approved/certified", "fully booked in 30 days"
- "You will make $X in your first month" (any specific dollar figure tied to outcome)
- "Easy", "effortless", "no work" — cohosting is work

## Asset 1: FREE PDF — "21 AI Prompts Every Airbnb Host Should Have"

### Cover (page 1)
- Top: small HOSTFLOW mark (navy on white)
- Center headline: **21 AI Prompts Every Airbnb Host Should Have** (Helvetica Bold, 28pt, navy #0B1220)
- Subtitle: "Prompts for guest messages, pricing, listing audits, owner outreach, and the 2am panic texts. Use them in Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI assistant."
- Bottom: hostflow.me + © 2026

### Body structure (21 prompts grouped by 5 categories)
Each prompt has 4 parts:
1. **When to use it** (1 line)
2. **The prompt** (in monospace, copy-paste ready)
3. **Why it works** (2 lines)
4. **Variation** (1 line — how to make it yours)

Categories:
- **Guest messages (5 prompts):** inquiry response, pre-arrival, mid-stay check-in, post-stay thank-you, bad-review recovery
- **Pricing & demand (4 prompts):** dynamic pricing review, comp-set audit, weekend-vs-weeknight analysis, last-minute gap filler
- **Listing audit (4 prompts):** 15-point audit, photo critique, description rewrite, SEO keyword check
- **Owner outreach (5 prompts):** cold pitch, follow-up after no reply, discovery-call opener, audit delivery, monthly statement draft
- **The 2am problems (3 prompts):** guest lockout, neighbor complaint, damage claim first response

### Page count: 8-12 pages
### Cover-back: "If these prompts helped, the $49 HostFlow STR Starter System has the full operating playbook — 10 modules, 50+ SOPs, contracts, dynamic pricing templates. → hostflow.me/str-starter"
