## Research summary

### Cohosting Acquisition (the $2-3k mentor competitor)
- Positioning: "Land your first client in 90 days" / "The easiest way to break into Airbnb without owning property"
- Offer: $2-3k mentorship with live weekly coaching + acquisition system
- Claimed proof: 173+ clients, 7-figure revenue generated for students, "10+ properties under management in 6 months"
- Format: VSL → free guide → sales call
- Hook they use: "Escape the paycheck-to-paycheck cycle. Replace your paycheck, keep your freedom."
- What they sell: Acquisition coaching (how to land co-hosting clients). Different from us — they sell "getting clients," we sell "running the operations."

### Oh She Hosts (the high-end service competitor)
- Positioning: "Hosting strategies for better stays" / "quality over quantity"
- Founder: Simone Strand, cabins / STR business builder
- Offer tier: Strategy session ($X), Hosting Collab ($X, 1:1 guided plan), Listing Makeover (one-time audit)
- Free lead magnet: "4 Simple Airbnb Listing Tweaks for More Bookings"
- Format: Lead magnet → email list → paid services
- Hook: "I went from a pick-up camper to a one-way ticket to the tropics. Here's how to build what works."
- What they sell: Listing optimization + 1:1 consulting. We don't compete directly; she sells services, we sell products.

### What we swipe

| From Cohosting Acquisition | From Oh She Hosts |
|---|---|
| "Land your first client" — keep the *first client* framing in our copy | "Quality over quantity" — same positioning stance |
| "Without owning property" — use as a counter-positioning line | "Hosting strategies for better stays" — angle of "strategies not hacks" |
| "Replace your paycheck" — use the *9-to-5 escape* hook for the $49 system | "4 simple tweaks" — pattern: free guide that delivers a small win → leads to bigger product |
| Acquisition system framing | Lead magnet → paid product funnel |

### Differentiation we claim (and can defend)

- **Lower entry price ($49 vs $2-3k)** — broader TAM, faster impulse purchase
- **"AI prompts" angle** — uniquely ours; the competitor set doesn't lean into AI
- **PDF-first delivery** — instant gratification vs "wait for the next cohort"
- **Operations focus** — we sell the *running* of cohosting, not the *finding* of clients (theirs) and not the *single-listing optimization* (theirs)
