How it unfolds

From kit to first paying client in 30 days.

The system follows a 3-phase loop: position → pitch → operate. Each phase has a Day-1 to Day-N script.

PHASE 01 / DAY 1

Position.

Write your 1-page brief: niche, geography, owner ICP, fee structure, differentiation. Day 2 draft your contract. Day 3 build your 30-name prospect list.

PHASE 02 / WEEK 2

Pitch.

Send 5 cold pitches per day for 10 days. Use the 15-point audit scorecard. Run discovery calls. Walk the audit on screen-share. Send the audit + proposal within 48 hours.

PHASE 03 / WEEK 3-4

Operate.

Close first client. Run the 30-minute kickoff. Install the cleaning rotation. Adjust pricing daily. Send the first monthly statement on the 5th. Earn your first $400+ cohost fee by month-end.
